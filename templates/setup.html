Developer Integration Guide
Step 1: Open Developer Console

Access the official Wyze Developer Portal: https://developer-api-console.wyze.com/

Step 2: Sign In

Log in using your standard Wyze mobile app email address and account password.

Step 3: Generate API Credentials

Navigate to the **API Credentials** section in the dashboard and click the **"Create key"** button.

Step 4: Retrieve ID & Secret

Copy the newly generated fields and paste them here:
• The **Key ID** corresponds to your **API Client ID**.
• The **Key Secret** corresponds to your **API Developer Key**.

💡 Troubleshooting P2P IP Streams

AURA connects directly to Wyze cameras inside your home network using native WebRTC peer-to-peer pipelines for ultra-low latency. Make sure your cameras are online and turned on in your Wyze application.
